# Data methodology

This is a candidate research dataset, not a verified bankruptcy register. Shutdown, product discontinuation, acquisition, restructuring, administration and formal insolvency are distinct outcomes. The initial source URL is a discovery starting point, not proof for each record. Verify every claim against company announcements, court/insolvency records, regulators or reputable reporting.

## Location policy
Founding location and last known headquarters should be separate fields. Coordinates must refer to a documented historical address, not a city centroid. Unknown coordinates remain null. Record coordinate source and precision.

## Evidence standard
Add sources for legal status, closure date, failure reasons and historical address. Record publication/access dates and confidence.
